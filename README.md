# Digitising Agriculture & ESG Data: ETG (Export Trading Group)

> **Organisation:** ETG, Digitise Agriculture · **Sector:** Agriculture & commodity trading · **Reach:** Pan-African farmer networks → global brands

## The challenge

ETG's farm-level operations ran **on paper**, and the records were later typed into Excel. That made the data slow to collect, hard to verify and almost impossible to share with downstream buyers. At the same time, global fashion brands buying the raw materials were asking for credible **ESG and sustainability evidence** from the farms that supply them.

## What I built

**1. From paper to digital operations**
- Replaced paper and spreadsheet processes with digital capture at the source
- Standardised the data so it could be validated, aggregated and reported

**2. An ESG & sustainability data app**
- Created an app to capture ESG and sustainability data in the field
- Turned farm-level data into reporting that is **shared with clients including Hugo Boss, LVMH Group and other global fashion brands**, giving them traceable evidence from their supply chain

**3. Regenerative farming at scale**
- Used the data and reporting to drive and track the adoption of **regenerative farming practices** among farmers

**4. A farmer–producer marketplace opportunity**
- The digital farmer network opened up a new **marketplace** connecting farmers and producers, for example supplying farming inputs and materials

## Outcomes

- Operations moved from paper and Excel to digital, structured data
- ESG and sustainability reporting now reaches global brand clients, including Hugo Boss and LVMH Group
- Regenerative farming practices driven and measured on the ground
- A new commercial opportunity: a marketplace between farmers and producers
